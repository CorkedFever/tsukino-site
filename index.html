01 — About

A small company, built to move like one.

Tsukino Software Solutions is an Ohio software studio founded in 2026: direct, accountable engineering from first conversation to production deploy.

The name comes from the Japanese for "of the moon." It's a nod to working with patience and precision, the way the moon moves through its phases: steady, predictable, always coming back around to full.

The studio runs two tracks: contract software development for clients, and independent products of its own.
