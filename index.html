01 — About

A small company, built to move like one.

Tsukino Software Solutions is an Ohio-based LLC founded in 2026. No layers, no handoffs: direct, accountable engineering from first conversation to production deploy.

The name comes from the Japanese for "of the moon." It's a nod to working with patience and precision, the way the moon moves through its phases: steady, predictable, always coming back around to full.

The company runs two tracks: contract software development for clients, and an in-house product built to solve a real problem: job searching that actually respects your time.
